## Top Conversion Blockers

- Choice overload: 25+ priced à-la-carte items with terse jargon names and no 'recommended for my business' preset overwhelm busy, anxious, and first-time buyers.
- No human safety net at the purchase point — no 'talk to someone first', tooltips, or guidance for buyers afraid of picking the wrong bundle.
- Reassurance ('Previewed before you pay', cancel-anytime, refunds, ownership) is buried in the footer instead of surfaced where money is committed.
- Total-cost ambiguity: setup vs. /mo is small inline text and there's no first-year total, alarming budget buyers (esp. Package C at $550/mo).
- Zero proof on the page — no portfolio, reviews, results, or credentials to justify prices or the repeated 'cheaper than building it yourself' claim.
- Thin per-item scope — service names don't explain what you actually get, so value can't be evaluated or compared.

## Top Trust Gaps

- No social proof (reviews, testimonials, named clients, portfolio) anywhere on the configurator.
- No refund/guarantee/cancellation reassurance at the point of purchase (footer-only).
- No privacy/data-handling statement despite selling email, CRM, lead-routing, and follow-up that touch customer PII.
- Unsubstantiated comparative claim ('cheaper than building it yourself') repeated three times with no math.
- No process / 'what happens after I pay' / turnaround commitment.
- No owner face, story, or local identity to make a solo studio feel real and accountable.
