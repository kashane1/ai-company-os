## Top Conversion Blockers

- No proof of any kind on the pricing page — no portfolio, results, testimonials, or credentials — so trust collapses at the moment of payment.
- Monthly value is never itemized; recurring fees ($50–$550/mo) read as a subscription cost rather than a defined service, and the 'optional monthly support' intro contradicts the bundled monthly fees.
- Feature labels are unexplained jargon ('Review Readiness System', 'Done-for-you Setup', 'Conversion Lab', 'teardown', 'preflight') despite the page claiming pricing 'stays clear'.
- The $90/mo (B) to $550/mo (C) jump causes sticker shock and confuses whether Google Ads spend is included or extra.
- Risk-reversal ('Previewed before you pay', cancellation terms) is buried in footer text instead of next to the buy buttons.
- No post-click clarity: buyers can't tell if 'Start this package' charges them now or starts a no-payment build.

## Top Trust Gaps

- Zero social proof: no reviews, client logos, named businesses, or case studies.
- No location or service area, so the 'small local studio' positioning has nothing to stand on.
- Maintenance/monthly scope undefined (no edit limits, no response SLA, no cancellation clarity at point of sale).
- No founder face/story to humanize the solo-operator advantage that the page only mentions in fine print.
- No data-ownership / handover reassurance (do I keep my domain and site if I leave?).
- No differentiation vs DIY builders or freelancers to justify the price premium.
