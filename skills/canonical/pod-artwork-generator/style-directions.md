# POD style directions

Use these as optional starting points, not a fixed rotation. Match the idea and
product; introduce another original treatment when it fits better. Preserve
clear silhouettes, readable type, and transparent-background constraints.

| Family | Useful when | Visual traits | Print caution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retro mascot / halftone | Playful puns, food, animals, nostalgic humor | Bold outlined mascot, limited warm palette, halftone grain, chunky display lettering | Keep dots and small features large enough to survive print. |
| Cute character | Wholesome, cozy, or silly concepts | Expressive character, rounded shapes, sticker-like outline, simple props | Avoid overcrowding cute details; keep facial features and copy large. |
| Monochrome engraving | Dry humor, natural history, archival or understated ideas | High-contrast ink lines, hatch shading, restrained or single-color treatment | Test fine hatching and light ink against the garment color. |
| Ornate gothic | Witchy, mystical, dark-romantic themes | Ornamental frame, dramatic serif type, ravens, florals, celestial motifs, jewel-like accents | Preserve negative space; dense black detail can close up on dark garments. |
| Photoreal cutout | Food, objects, or tactile realism are the point | Isolated, believable subject with carefully cleaned edges and controlled highlights | Confirm genuine alpha and prevent a photographed backdrop or halo. |
| Cinematic science / fact graphic | Curious facts, space, weather, or bold educational humor | Dramatic focal image, luminous contrast, hierarchy-led factual type | Do not let cinematic effects create a rectangular scene background; fact text needs high contrast. |
| Type-forward minimal | The phrase itself is the product | Strong custom lettering, sparse icons, crisp hierarchy, limited palette | Verify exact wording and legibility at intended print size; do not rely on thin type. |

Use the family names only as shorthand. Explain each proposed direction in its
own card, including why it suits the request and how its product composition
will differ from the other cards.
