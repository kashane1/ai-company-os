### Context

Describe the life situation that makes this person consider the service.

### Jobs To Be Done

What progress is the buyer trying to make?

### Fears And Objections

List the objections that would stop the buyer from taking action.

### Trust Signals

List the proof, credentials, details, reviews, policies, and page elements that
would make the buyer more comfortable.

### Dealbreakers

List the claims, missing details, or frictions that would make the buyer leave.

### Decision Process

Describe how this buyer compares providers, who else influences the decision,
and what they need before contacting the business.

### Language They Use

Capture phrases and plain-language concerns the buyer would actually say.

### What Makes Them Act

Describe the moment where the page has done enough to make the buyer call,
book, submit a form, or reply.

### What Makes Them Leave

Describe the moment where the buyer decides the page is not worth another minute.

### Review Prompt

When reviewing a page, embody this buyer and answer:

1. What is clear?
2. What feels vague or untrustworthy?
3. What objection is unresolved?
4. Would you call, book, submit the form, or leave?
5. What copy would make the next action easier?

Always include the caveat that this is a synthetic simulation, not a real customer
interview.
