# Outreach Copy Rules

Cold outreach should sound like one person writing to another person.

## Hard Rules

- Never use an em dash in outreach copy.
- Do not write like a generated sales email.
- Do not open with exact review counts unless the operator intentionally adds one.
- Do not say "clearly," "game changer," "no brainer," "unlock," "transform," or similar generic sales language.
- Do not overstate the problem. Say what you noticed, not what must be true.
- Keep first touches short enough that a business owner can read them between customers.
- Use plain punctuation: periods, commas, question marks, and the occasional colon.
- One soft ask per message.

## Preferred Shape

1. Say how you found them.
2. Mention one concrete thing you noticed.
3. Offer the private preview.
4. Ask if they want to take a look.

## Example

Hi there. I came across Bliss Bakery while looking up bakeries around Los Angeles. I put together a private one-page website preview for you. Is it okay if I send it over?
