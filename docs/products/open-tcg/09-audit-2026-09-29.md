# OpenTCG radar — audit and prioritized backlog (2026-09-29)

> **TL;DR:** The radar runs reliably and fails safe on the surface, but it can **silently miss the alerts it exists for**, and it had one real security bug.
>
> **Fixed during the audit:** the IMAP connection didn't verify Gmail's certificate. The inbox address was scrubbed from test fixtures, and doc drift was fixed.
>
> **Five P1s come next:**
> - alert on set-only "LIVE" posts: both 30th Celebration Walmart drawings were missed, and another opens Sep 30 at 2 PM PT
> - gate freshness on arrival time: some posts arrive 22–35 min late
> - make ingestion crash-proof: one odd email can stall the inbox forever
> - harden untrusted links and senders: SSRF was proven in a proof of concept
> - alert when a source goes down
>
> Coverage is the biggest product gap. In 3 weeks the free sources gave 28 TCG posts and no live Target or Pokémon Center restocks, and no episode has been reported by two sources.
>
> Backlog: `todos/098`–`118`. The working order is below.

## Scope and method

The radar in `products/open-tcg/` was reviewed from four angles:

1. **Code correctness and reliability.** An independent review ran the test suite, used read-only DB queries, and simulated failures with a fake socket and mailbox.
2. **Security.** An independent review built offline proofs of concept against the real code, with loopback servers and stubbed send/open.
3. **Data.** What the radar actually recorded between 2026-09-09 and 09-29.
4. **Operations and plan gaps.** The LaunchAgents, the scheduled sign-in check, logs, and build-out 1's promises against what exists.

## What is working

- **Jobs:** all three LaunchAgents have run continuously since Sep 26, with zero failures and empty error logs. Sync polls only due sources: PokeBeach every 15 min, Southern Hobby daily, tcgcsv after its 20:30 UTC rebuild. The Mac never sleeps (`pmset sleep 0`).
- **Sign-in check:** the 6 AM check ran on schedule. On Sep 28, Costco, Walmart, Target and Amazon were signed in and Pokémon Center was signed out.
- **"When" detection:** it works. For example, it caught Southern Hobby moving the Mega Forces Tin from Aug 28 to Dec 4, and product news such as the Delta Reign prerelease promos.
- **Matching:** it works on real posts, including "ETB" and "SPC" abbreviations and multi-product posts.
- **Storage:** small and healthy (DB 576 KB, logs under 25 KB).
- **Already safe:** SQL is parameterized. osascript gets its text as arguments. IMAP is EXAMINE + BODY.PEEK only. `lands_on` rejects lookalike domains. No secrets are in git history.

## How we keep improving

- **Work order:** take the lowest-numbered open P1 in the table, then the P2s in order. Each ticket is test-first, lands in one commit, and closes by moving to `todos/archive/` with a completion note.
- **Real data drives tickets:** every missed or wrong alert becomes a regression test from the real observation row.
- **Weekly review:** check the scorecard, source volume and lag, Guppy-logged drops the radar missed, parser surprises in new data, and health. Re-rank this table and file new tickets.
- **Re-audit monthly,** or after any new source is added.
- **Metrics to watch:**
  - watched-drop recall: Guppy-logged or manually confirmed drops the radar alerted on, over all known drops
  - alert latency (median and p90)
  - false alerts (from `radar mark`)
  - source uptime
  - episodes with two or more sources
