## Gmail forwarding filter

The radar reads the project inbox (`jimsimons01102000@gmail.com`) every 30 seconds, read-only. To let it see store mail sent to your address, set up forwarding in **ksakhakorn@gmail.com**:

1. Gmail → Settings → **Forwarding and POP/IMAP** → **Add a forwarding address** → `jimsimons01102000@gmail.com`. Gmail sends a confirmation code to that address. Ask Claude to read it from the project inbox, then enter it.
2. In the Gmail search box, paste the query below → **Create filter** → **Forward it to** `jimsimons01102000@gmail.com`. Also tick **Never send it to Spam**, and apply a label such as "TCG drops" if you like.

```
from:(cardboardanddie.com OR knightanddaygames.com OR larrysgamestore.com OR mhdealsplus.com OR pokemonplug.com OR tcghubandhobbies.com OR thewarpgate.net OR wolfdentcg.com OR wulfgaming.com OR allpoketcg.com OR blackwolfcollectibles.com OR capyquesttcg.com OR cardcaverntradingcards.com OR cardlandmarket.com OR cashcardsunlimited.com OR common-lands.com OR cromatcg.com OR fabricatorsforge.com OR flipsidegaming.com OR galactictoys.com OR gamersgrove.com OR gghobbycard.com OR hobby-addicts.com OR hobbycardshop.com OR impactgamingcenter.gg OR jafcomics.com OR jarocards.com OR joshscards.com OR kollectkorner.com OR loosepacks.com OR mysterymtg.com OR n4ytcg.com OR nerdzoic.com OR nexustabletopgames.com OR overthebrick.com OR poke-collect.com OR pokemalltcg.com OR realgoodeal.com OR roboskillz.com OR safari-zone.com OR skyboxct.com OR stackedpax.com OR stompinggroundstcg.com OR stonebrookcollectibles.com OR tcghobby.com OR tcg-stadium.com OR tcgora.com OR thetrainercourt.com OR ultimasupply.com OR vegas.singles OR vgmx.com OR zulusgames.com OR beckettcastletcg.com OR forgeandfiregaming.com OR levelupgamesatl.com OR tcgmoonblast.com OR tcgslayer.io OR thirdspacetcg.com)

As those emails arrive, `radar store-mail-filter` adds their senders, so re-run it and update the filter after you add new signups or notice store mail that isn't being forwarded.
