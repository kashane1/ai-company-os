# Task Packet: GTM And Launch Asset Preparation

## Packet ID

`after-plans-supervisor-003`

## Lane

`supervisor`

## Risk

`medium`

## Approval

Drafting only. External communications or spend actions require separate approval.

## Tests

Not required for this planning packet.

## Objective

Prepare the founder-supervised launch materials for seeded contexts without creating a new uncontrolled growth lane.

## Inputs

- `../GTM_PLAN.md`
- `../LAUNCH_PLAN.md`
- `../APP_STORE_POSITIONING.md`
- `../OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`

## Scope

- choose initial seeded contexts
- draft organizer or ambassador materials
- define launch metrics and success checks
- list founder approvals needed before outreach

## Out Of Scope

- running ads
- sending outreach
- automating GTM operations

## Deliverables

- seeded context shortlist
- founder-facing launch checklist
- draft outreach message set
- measurement and learning plan

## Constraints

- keep GTM tied to real recurring contexts
- do not widen into public marketplace behavior
- keep this work as documents and manual execution aids

## Exit Criteria

- the founder can see exactly what launch preparation remains
- seeded launch work is separable from product and App Store execution
- no new worker lane is required

## Resume Point

Start by choosing one to three dense launch contexts and writing the founder checklist that must be approved before any outreach starts.
