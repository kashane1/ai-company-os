# Product Brief: After Plans

## Product Thesis

After Plans is an iPhone-first continuation app that helps people turn one real-world activity into the next one while shared context is still alive.

The product wins if it feels:

- easier than texting everyone
- safer than random stranger meetup apps
- lower-pressure than formal event planning
- more useful in the moment than group chat

## Core Promise

When the current thing ends, open After Plans to see what is next and join it without making the moment awkward.

## Target User

Primary user:

- a socially open person who is more comfortable joining than initiating
- often participates in shared real-world activities
- values low-pressure coordination over heavy planning
- prefers people they know, are already around, or have context with over pure strangers

## User Job

Primary job:

- reduce friction between "that was fun" and "what are we doing next?"

Secondary jobs:

- signal interest without overcommitting
- find a continuation with bounded trust
- keep momentum going after dinners, meetups, classes, services, conferences, or hangouts

## Value Proposition

- faster than organizing a new thread
- more situational than event marketplaces
- safer than anonymous local social apps
- more focused than broad social products

## Product Rules

- iPhone-first
- continuation moment first
- join-first behavior first
- shared context outranks public discovery
- known people, same-context people, and prior partners outrank strangers
- no hard consumer paywall in v1
- no dating posture
- no anonymous random-chat posture
- trust and moderation are core product requirements, not later polish

## V1 Promise

Make it easy to see, start, and join what is happening next right after a real-world activity with people you know or are already around.

## MVP Includes

- lightweight identity and profile
- context selection tied to the activity that just happened
- ranked after-plan feed
- three creation modes
- soft participation actions
- plan lifecycle states
- share link and QR invite flows
- bounded visibility controls
- report, block, and moderation basics

## MVP Excludes

- public map-wide discovery
- anonymous chat
- heavy profiles and follower graphs
- dating framing
- DMs before plan context exists
- payments or ticketing
- large-group event hosting
- organizer CRM

## Monetization Direction

- free consumer core in v1
- organizer or community premium later
- selective consumer premium only if repeated value becomes clear

## Success Criteria

The product is succeeding when users can:

- open the app after an activity and immediately understand what it is for
- see relevant continuation options rooted in shared context
- join or signal interest with one low-friction action
- turn ambiguous interest into a confirmed next plan
- feel safer because visibility is bounded and identity is not anonymous

North-star direction:

- repeat confirmed after-plans per active user in recurring contexts
