## Core Bet

Build an iPhone-first social continuation app for the moment right after a real-world activity, when people are still nearby, shared context is fresh, and the next plan often dies from awkwardness or coordination friction.

- Not event planning
- Not a stranger meetup product
- Not a dating app
- Not a broad social network
- Not a group chat replacement

The wedge is simple:

- make the next plan easier right after the current one ends
- help people join with low pressure
- use shared context and familiar people to reduce trust friction

## Founder Thesis

After Plans can win by becoming the easiest way to keep a good real-world moment going with people you know or are already around, without making it socially hard.

## Product Position

Category:

- social continuation
- post-plan coordination

Brand stance:

- lively
- momentum-driven
- low-pressure
- human

Product feel:

- immediate instead of formal
- join-first instead of creator-heavy
- identity-light but not anonymous
- bounded-context instead of broad public discovery

## Target User

Primary users:

- socially active young adults in dense in-person environments
- people who often attend shared activities and are more willing to join than initiate
- loose friend groups or semi-recurring communities without a strong coordination layer

Best early contexts:

- meetups and hobby gatherings
- classes and campus events
- post-dinner or post-drinks continuation
- conferences
- recurring local communities

## Core User Jobs

- see what is happening next right after this
- signal interest without sending awkward texts
- join something already forming
- turn weak intent into a real plan
- keep momentum going after a shared activity

## Product Principles

- shared context beats cold-start discovery
- joining is easier than creating
- soft signals come before hard commitment
- plans should visibly mature over time
- texting handles last-mile chatter, not the whole discovery layer
- known people and past plan partners outrank strangers
- bounded context outranks broad public discovery

## Core Loop

1. a shared activity ends
2. the user opens After Plans
3. the user sees or creates an after-plan
4. nearby or same-context people join or signal interest
5. the plan confirms
6. a good outcome strengthens the habit of opening the app after future activities

## MVP Requirements

Must-have product elements from the founder package:

- lightweight account and profile
- current-context or nearby activity anchoring
- three plan creation modes: exact plan, default option, open intent
- plan lifecycle states: Open, Forming, Confirmed, Active, Closed
- ranked discovery feed
- join, interested, and suggest place actions
- visible participation counts
- share link and QR flow
- reporting, blocking, moderation basics

Important but explicitly secondary to the first loop:

- known-people ranking
- past-plan history
- suggested nearby venues
- on-my-way or running-late execution polish

## Trust And Safety Stance

The founder package is clear that day-one trust matters:

- identity-light but not anonymous
- no anonymous random-chat posture
- bounded visibility
- reporting and blocking from day one
- moderation posture from day one
- careful location-permission timing
- age and eligibility assumptions must be explicit before launch

## Monetization Stance

- v1 consumer core is free
- no hard consumer paywall in v1
- organizer or community premium comes later
- selective consumer premium is only for later, if repeated value becomes obvious

## App Store And Compliance Posture

- Social Networking is the likely primary category
- App Review risk rises if the product feels like anonymous stranger meetup or random chat
- claims should emphasize continuation, coordination, and low-pressure joining
- claims should avoid promising guaranteed social outcomes or safety absolutes

## Key Risks

- cold start and liquidity
- users defaulting to texting
- episodic retention
- trust perception drifting toward stranger meetup or dating
- App Review issues around UGC, location, anonymity, or weak moderation
- scope creep into event platform, social network, or organizer CRM

## Closing Recommendation

Build After Plans as a join-first post-activity continuation app with:

- a free consumer core
- bounded-context trust model
- lightweight but real identity
- a narrow continuation loop that proves people open the app right after something ends and successfully continue the moment
