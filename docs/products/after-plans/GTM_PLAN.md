# GTM Plan: After Plans

## GTM Goal

Seed After Plans in contexts where repeated real-world gatherings already exist and the continuation moment happens often enough to create habit.

## Positioning Ladder

Functional:

- see and join what is next

Emotional:

- do not let the moment end there

Identity:

- be the person who keeps good momentum going

## Primary ICP

- socially active young adults
- recurring in-person communities
- dense local contexts where people frequently decide what to do next
- users more willing to join than initiate

## Best Initial Contexts

- hobby meetups
- campus organizations and classes
- recurring city social events
- conferences
- post-dinner or post-drinks groups

## Messaging Architecture

Top message:

- Make plans after plans.

Support lines:

- See what is happening after.
- Join what is next with people you know or are already around.
- Turn "we should do something" into an actual plan.
- Less awkward than texting everyone.

## Channel Strategy

Start with:

- organizer-seeded communities
- campus ambassadors
- micro-creators in local social scenes
- QR and share-link distribution inside real-world moments

Do not start with:

- broad paid acquisition
- public marketplace positioning
- city-wide stranger discovery

## Growth Mechanics

- share link opens
- QR scans in person
- invite-driven installs
- repeat use in recurring contexts
- prior partner recognition and social memory

## GTM Risks

- users default to texting
- the app is too vague without live examples
- the product is mistaken for dating or stranger meetup
- seeded contexts are too sparse to demonstrate value

## Mitigations

- keep the story tied to the after moment
- seed a few dense contexts well instead of many loosely
- show bounded-trust cues everywhere
- keep the consumer core free

## GTM Deliverables

- landing-page or App Store copy set
- screenshot story
- organizer pitch note for seeded communities
- ambassador talking points
- seeded launch checklist
