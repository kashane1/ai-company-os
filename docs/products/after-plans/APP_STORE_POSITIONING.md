# App Store Positioning: After Plans

## Positioning Summary

After Plans should present as a join-first social continuation app for the moment after a real-world activity ends.

The listing should answer:

- what problem it solves
- when to use it
- why it feels lower-pressure and safer than stranger-meetup products

## Category Recommendation

Primary category:

- Social Networking

Rationale:

- the product is about social coordination, not event ticketing or productivity
- this category better matches continuation and social formation than Lifestyle

## Title And Subtitle

App name:

- After Plans

Subtitle candidates:

- Make plans after plans
- See what is next after
- Join what is happening after

## Messaging Rules

Lead with:

- the after moment
- low-pressure joining
- people you know or are already around
- continuation rather than broad discovery

Avoid:

- dating-adjacent language
- friend-finder language
- anonymous local social framing
- overclaiming safety

## Review Notes To Support Submission

If App Review needs context, emphasize:

- the app is for post-activity continuation
- users are shown context-bounded plans, not an open public stranger feed
- the app includes reporting, blocking, and bounded visibility
- the app does not center anonymous chat
