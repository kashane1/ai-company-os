## Common questions

### How do I report a plan or a person?

Open the plan, tap the safety icon, and choose Report. Pick the closest reason and add a note if you want. Reports go straight into our moderation queue.

You can also reach the safety center from the profile screen at any time. Reports made in-app and reports made by email both reach the same queue.

### How do I block someone?

From any plan they appear in, tap their row and choose Block. The block takes effect immediately. You will no longer see their plans, their participation in other plans, or any invites they share. They will no longer see anything you host or participate in.

To unblock someone, open the safety center and remove the entry from your block list.

### Why don't I see any plans in my feed?

After Plans is bounded by context. You see plans from contexts you are part of (a class, a meetup, a community), from people you have planned with before, or from direct invites. If your feed is empty, it usually means no one in your bounded contexts has started a plan recently.

If you think your feed should have something but does not, email us with the context and approximate time and we will look.

### Where is my data and can you delete it?

What we hold and how we use it is described in [PRIVACY_POLICY.md](PRIVACY_POLICY.md). To request account deletion, email the support address from the email tied to your account (or, if you signed in anonymously, include your in-app first name and the rough date you started using the app). We confirm and complete deletions within 30 days.

### I think I found a bug.

Please email us with:

- the device and iOS version you are on (Settings → General → About),
- the app version (Settings → After Plans, or the About screen in-app),
- what you were doing when it happened,
- a screenshot or screen recording if you have one.

We do not run third-party analytics or crash reporters, so we rely on what you tell us.

### I have a feature request.

Send it to the support address. We read everything and reply when we can. We are intentionally building a small, focused app, so not every request will land in the product — but every one helps us understand what is missing.

### How do I delete my account?

Email the support address from the email tied to your account. We confirm and complete account deletion within 30 days. Plans you joined that other people host will remain, with your participation row removed.
