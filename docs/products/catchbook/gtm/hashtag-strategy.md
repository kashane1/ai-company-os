# Catchbook — Hashtag Strategy

Per-platform tag sets, refreshed from niche research. The `social-post-safety`
validator checks that published posts match one of these sets.

Hashtags are grouped by niche. Platform limits apply per-post, not per-section.
Pick from the appropriate niche group + brand tags for each post.

## Notes

- TikTok: Avoid broad tag stuffing. Algorithm now de-emphasizes more than 5
  tags. Best practice is 1 broad (#fishing) + 1 community (#fishtok) + 2-3
  niche/topic tags.
- Instagram: 8-15 tags optimal for 2025-2026 algorithm. Mix 2-3 broad with
  5+ niche tags. Posts with optimized hashtag strategy see 12-30% higher
  engagement.
- Threads: Keep to 3. Platform rewards conversational content over tag
  discovery.
- #catchbook / #catchbookapp reserved for Campaign One (post-launch). Do not
  use in Campaign Zero (pre-launch audience building).
