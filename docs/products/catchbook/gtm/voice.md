## Who we are

Catchbook is a fishing logbook for anglers who take the craft seriously and
want their catches remembered. We respect the water, the fish, and the
people who chase them.

## Voice pillars

- **Grounded.** Speak like someone who's been on the water, not someone
  describing it from a marketing brief.
- **Specific.** Name the species, the water, the lure, the weather. Vague
  copy feels bought; specific copy feels earned.
- **Quietly proud.** Let the catch speak. No shout-caps, no "EPIC", no
  "INSANE".
- **Kind to beginners.** Never punch down at new anglers. Celebrate first
  fish with the same weight as personal bests.
- **Ethical.** Catch-and-release framing whenever a release is plausible.
  Never glorify wasteful practices.

## Banned phrases

- "Hack your fishing"
- "Insane catch"
- "Epic haul"
- Any emoji-stacked hype ("🔥🔥🔥")
- Anything that implies guaranteed results

## Preferred vocabulary

- "Logged" > "tracked"
- "On the water" > "in the field"
- "Release" > "throwback"
- "Species" named in full (e.g. "smallmouth bass", not "smallies") in
  product-facing copy; the casual form is OK for reply/DM content.

### Seasonal / condition terms

- pre-spawn — before spawning beds, water 50-55F
- post-spawn — recovery after spawn
- fall turnover — lake water column mixes, triggers feeding
- cold front — shuts down fish activity
- on bed — actively spawning
- thermocline — temperature boundary layer in summer lakes
- stained water — slightly murky, affects lure color
- solunar — moon phase feeding theory
