## Removed in v2

- `appPreviews` — placeholder SF-Symbol icons, off-brand. The persistent mascot header replaces its function.
- `visibilityFraming` — abstract framing ("Visibility is the system that works") that doesn't match copy used anywhere else in the app. `meetYourClock` and `personalizeIntro` cover the same ground.
- `entryView` (2026-05-07) — the post-paywall "Setting up your clock…" placeholder. `paywallPrimary.onClose` writes the profile and `RootView`'s @Query swaps to `MainTabView` on the next runloop tick, so the placeholder was a one-frame safety-net that didn't earn its place.
