# Life Clock Founder Pack

Working title: **Life Clock**

This founder pack defines a new iPhone-first Health & Fitness app concept: a longevity game where daily habits move a user's projected Life Clock. It is designed as a safer, more App Store-friendly evolution of the death-clock idea: emotionally powerful, but framed around agency, healthspan, and time earned rather than a deterministic death prediction.

## Product one-liner

**Life Clock is a HealthKit-powered longevity game that shows how daily habits change your projected healthspan, then gives users small quests to earn time back.**

## Main strategic call

Do not ship a literal medical death predictor. Ship a Health & Fitness subscription product with a game mechanic. The app should make mortality motivating, not paralyzing.
