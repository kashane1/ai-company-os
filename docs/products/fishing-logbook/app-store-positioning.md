# App Store Positioning: Private Fishing Logbook

## Positioning

Private Fishing Logbook is a private performance companion for anglers who want to remember what worked without sharing their spots.

## Category Direction

- primary: Sports
- secondary: Reference

## Messaging Angle

- private, not social
- remember what worked
- fast logging on the water
- confidence before the next trip
- share the catch, not the spot

## Name Direction

Working directions, not final names:

- Catchbook
- Tidelog
- Angler Journal
- Spotbook Fishing

## Subtitle Direction

- Private Catch & Spot Log
- Remember What Worked
- Fishing Journal & Insights

## Screenshot Story

1. Log catches in seconds
2. Keep your spots private
3. See what worked last time
4. Track conditions automatically
5. Review spot-by-spot history
6. Share a win without exposing the spot

## Metadata Notes

- lean into privacy, repeat waters, and recall
- avoid promising AI
- avoid promising community scale
- emphasize determinism and trusted personal history
- if sharing is shown, frame it as privacy-safe export sharing rather than social connection
