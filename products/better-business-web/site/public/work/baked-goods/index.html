What's in the case

Sweet, savory, and seriously soft.

A rotating case of breads, pastries, macarons and cakes — pillowy, fresh, and a little addictive. A few of the regulars below; the full case changes through the day.

Fan favorite
Savory

Sausage Bread

Soft, pillowy bread wrapped around a juicy, flavorful sausage — a regular family's all-time favorite breakfast.

Savory

The "Fire" Bread

The savory stuffed bread people come back for — pesto, sun-dried tomato and cheese baked into a golden, crackly crust.

Cakes

Pineapple Sponge & Chocolate Puff

Light pineapple sponge cake, and a chocolate cake with puff pastry layered inside. Showpiece whole cakes, too — fruit-topped, character and celebration cakes.

Sweet

Gourmet Macarons

Trays of them, in rotating flavors — rose, taro, coffee, blueberry-lavender and more. Boxed up to go in the botanical-print box.

Sweet

Breads, Buns & Mochi

Red bean, sweet potato, matcha, pumpkin and corn breads, plus mochi, danishes and muffins — soft and pillowy, with cream and fruit fillings.

Drinks

Matcha, Taro & Fruit Drinks

Cream-top drinks and fruit juices to go with it — the matcha and taro smoothies are a reliable pairing for anything from the case.

Items rotate and sell out — call (802) 555-0119 to check the case or order a whole cake ahead.

The space

Premium glow-up, in Little Tokyo.

The newly refreshed counter is all warm wood, white brick and good lighting — vintage KitchenAids and a record player on the shelf, neon on the wall. Longtime regulars call it one of the best changes the marketplace has seen.
