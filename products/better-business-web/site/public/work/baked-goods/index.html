Our story

Flour milled down the road. Bread baked before the village wakes.

Goldenrod Bakehouse sits in an old brick storefront on Mill Lane, where the ovens are warm by 4 a.m. We work in small batches with flour milled nearby, so the loaves taste like the season they were baked in.

No day-old discounts, no freezer cases. What we make, we sell that day — and start again tomorrow.

"Real butter, slow time, and good company. That's the whole recipe."

The room

A place to linger, not just grab-and-go.

Pull up a seat by the window with a flat white and a warm morning bun. Vinyl on the turntable, regulars at the counter, and the smell of fresh bread doing most of the talking.
