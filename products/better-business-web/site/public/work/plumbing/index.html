FAQ

Do you handle true emergencies — late nights and weekends?
Yes. TrueLine Plumbing & Sons is available 24 hours a day, every day. Plumbers have shown up at 10pm and worked past midnight to resolve an urgent leak. Same professionalism, any hour.
Will I know the cost before work starts?
Always. Your plumber will explain what's going on and give you the cost before any work begins — no guesses, no post-job surprises. Several customers note their final bill came in at or under the initial estimate.
What kinds of plumbing do you handle?
Leaks, drains, sewer lines, water heaters, faucets, toilets, sprinkler connections, valve work, and more. Both quick fixes and larger jobs like trenchless sewer repairs.
Do you do trenchless sewer repair?
Yes — in confirmed cases, the crew has cleared full sewer blockages without digging up the yard or basement. Ask about your specific situation when you call.
