Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need an appointment?

No appointment needed — walk-ins are welcome. If you're arriving close to closing time, calling ahead is a good idea to make sure they can take you.

Do you sell used tires as well as new?

Yes. Ironside Auto Works carries both new and used tires so you can choose what fits your budget. They'll show you the options and let you decide.

How long does a tire patch take?

Most patches are done in 10–15 minutes when the shop isn't slammed. Even when it's busy, customers report getting in and out in under 30 minutes.

Will they tell me if my tire can just be patched vs. replaced?

Yes — and they'll show you why. Customers frequently mention that the staff physically shows them the nail or damage so you can see the issue yourself before any decision is made.

What payment methods are accepted?

Credit cards, debit cards, and NFC tap-to-pay are all accepted. Cash is welcome too.

Is there wheelchair-accessible parking?

Yes. The shop has wheelchair-accessible parking and an accessible entrance.
