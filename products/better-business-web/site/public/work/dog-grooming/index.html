The Wagtail Grooming Studio difference

Nothing behind closed doors

"All grooming is done out in the open for everyone to see."

Most grooming salons hide their work in the back. At Wagtail Grooming Studio, every table is on the main floor. Owners walking past can see their pet being handled. It keeps the team accountable and keeps your dog calm — they can see you too.

Reviews consistently mention dogs arriving excited, not anxious. One client has kept a standing every-four-weeks appointment for over a year and gets the same great result every single time — because the team documents each dog's preferences.
