Data quality

The feed occasionally quotes a $600 card at $0.99. Left in, that fake 99% discount tops the list. This drops cards priced under 25% of their own 90-day median — a real decline drags its median down with it, a data error does not.

Many modern cards spike in release week and never return. Being 90% below that spike is normal, not a bargain.

TCGplayer holds the last price when nothing sells, so an illiquid card can quote one value for months and then step down hard. A price that never moves is a stale quote, not a stable market — and the high it is measured against may never have traded.
